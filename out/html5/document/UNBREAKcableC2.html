Understanding FM Frequencies:

To avoid interference when broadcasting audio through the device, users must find an unused station on their car's FM radio. The following will explain the difference between unused and active frequencies.

Unused "Static" Frequencies:

  • Empty radio stations that produce no vocals or music, just "static" or rustling, also referred to as white noise.
  • Using these stations when using an FM transmitter device minimizes audio interference and static, creating clear sound when streaming music, or using separate mobile device features (hands-free calling, navigation, and voice assistant controls)

Active Frequencies:

  • Active or working stations currently broadcasting a listenable audio signal used by local radio stations
  • "Busy" or active stations can cause static due to conflicting frequencies which is why they are not ideal when using the FM transmitter device.
