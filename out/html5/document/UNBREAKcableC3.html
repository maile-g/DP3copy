What are the Two Audio Inputs?

The two audio inputs this device provides are Bluetooth Mode and USB Flash Drive Mode. The following section defines and covers the differences between using these two inputs.

Bluetooth Mode

When in this mode, the FM transmitter wirelessly receives audio from your mobile/electronic device through Bluetooth connection and broadcasts it over an FM radio frequency. To learn to use this mode see, Using Bluetooth Mode (Audio Inputs)

Pros:

  • Provides convenient wireless streaming through mobile/electronic devices.
  • Offers hands-free calling and limits phone use for safer driving.

Cons:

  • Audio is compressed wirelessly which can cause a slight loss in "crispness" of sound.
  • Being a secondary wireless relay, (i.e.: Phone connects to transmitter which connects to car radio) it is more prone to interference or static.

USB Flash Drive Mode

In this mode, you can play music files stored on a USB thumb drive by plugging it into the USB Flash Drive Port on the FM transmitter. The device will read the music files and broadcast the audio over an FM radio frequency. To learn the steps for this mode see, Using USB Flash Drive Mode (Audio Inputs)

Pros:

  • Superior audio quality by directly playing locally stored audio files through USB, eliminating extra compression and distortion creating clearer sound.
  • More reliable connection over Bluetooth by providing a more stable audio feed.
  • Limits use of mobile/electronic devices, preserving battery life.

Cons:

  • Have to consistently maintain and update library of music files.
  • Potential of physical wear of the audio USB drive/port through repeated use.
  • Does not provide hands-free calling or wireless streaming features.
