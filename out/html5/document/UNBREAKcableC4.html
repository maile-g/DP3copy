Features of the Multi-Function Button:

The Multi-Function Button has several features for music-playing and Bluetooth calling that can be used for controlling music playback and managing phone calls, navigation, or voice assistant controls on your mobile device easier while driving.



Table 1. Key Features of the Multi-Function Button
Music-Playing Features: Bluetooth Calling Features:
  • Short Press 1x to play/pause song
  • Slide Left to skip to previous song
  • Slide Right to skip to the next song
  • Slide up to increase volume
  • Slide Down to lower volume
  • Short Press 1x to Answer/Hang-up call
  • Long Press 1x to Reject call
  • Short Press 2x to Redial the last call
  • Long press 1x to use Siri/Voice Controls (when not on a call or while being called)
