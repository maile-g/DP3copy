Important Notices and Cautions

This section will cover major cautions and notices when using this device.

  • Notice: FM signal of the device may weaken in certain locations (i.e. mountain regions, surrounded by buildings, or in tunnel). If that occurs, users should pair to a new unused frequency.
  • Notice: The transmitter will automatically connect to the pre-set mobile device every time it is turned on (If Bluetooth is activated on mobile device).
  • Notice: If there is an incoming call in music playing mode, the device will automatically switch telephone mode
  • Notice: If static noise continues after adjusting volumes and choosing an unused frequency, ensure no power lines are close to the device as they can cause electromagnetic radiation that can interfere and weaken the device's FM signal causing the background "buzz" in the audio.
  • Caution: LED display will flash when the car's battery is less than 12 Volt power, to reminding users to charge the car's battery.
  • Caution: To prevent damage to the device, do not use the transmitter in extreme high temperatures or in a humid, dusty, corrosive environment.
  • Caution: Keep the device unplugged from the car's cigarette lighter or power port when not in use for long periods of time.
  • Caution: Disassembling the device without due permission will void the warranty on the product.
