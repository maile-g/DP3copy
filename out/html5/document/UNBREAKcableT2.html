Using Bluetooth Mode (Audio Inputs)

Bluetooth mode allows for easy connectivity through your mobile/electronic device to stream music and use the hands-free calling features through the audio of an FM frequency on your car's radio.

After setting up the transmitter in your car, you can begin the process of connecting your mobile device to the FM transmitter using the default audio input, Bluetooth Mode.

  1. Go to the Bluetooth settings on your mobile device.
  2. Ensure Bluetooth is toggled on and wait for nearby or "other" devices to be scanned by your mobile device.
  3. Tip:
    If needed, use Password or Pin: 0000
    Look for the transmitter's device name "UBCH261" and tap on the name to connect
The mobile device will show that the transmitter is connected and a sound prompt from the transmitter will also notify that the Bluetooth connection is successful, and you can begin streaming audio from your connected device
