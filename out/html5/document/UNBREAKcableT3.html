Using USB Flash Drive Mode (Audio Inputs)

Using USB Flash Mode allows you to play downloaded music through a thumb drive in your car without needing to use a mobile device. The transmitter will read the music files and broadcast them over your car's radio

To use this mode, you will need to ensure you go through the set-up of the transmitter in your car. Once done, you can quickly begin the steps on using this input option.

  1. Locate the USB Flash Drive Port on your FM Transmitter.
  2. Trouble:
    If the transmitter stays in the default mode (Bluetooth), short press the Mode Switch Button once to switch to USB Flash Drive Mode.
    Plug in your USB thumb drive (loaded with supported formats) and plug into the music port.
    The transmitter should automatically detect the drive and begin playing music files.
  3. Use the Multi-Function Button music playing features to control music playback on the drive.
    See,Table 1 for details on the Multi-Function-Button's music-playing features
You are now successfully using USB Flash Drive Mode and can listen to your downloaded music in your car.
Important:
A limitation when using these modes is you cannot play audio from the thumb drive and a Bluetooth connected device at the same time. However, the device has Bluetooth priority, meaning when making calls or using voice controls, USB music files will automatically pause and switch to the connected devices' audio. When call ends or voice controls is no longer being used, USB music files will automatically resume.
